About & sources

I built Affinity as a quick reference for commonly used medications in psychiatry and anesthesiology. It's an educational prototype, not a substitute for the full prescribing information or your clinical judgment.

Sources. Dosing and boxed warnings: FDA prescribing information (DailyMed). Breastfeeding: LactMed. Older adults: AGS Beers Criteria (2023). Anesthesia doses: FDA labeling plus society guidance (ASRA for local anesthetic toxicity, MHAUS for malignant hyperthermia, SAMBA/ASA consensus for PONV), linked on each drug. Weight formulas: Devine (ideal body weight) and Janmahasatian 2005 (lean body weight). Side-effect ratings: qualitative estimates informed by labeling, Huhn et al. 2019 (Lancet; antipsychotics) and Cipriani et al. 2018 (Lancet; antidepressants). Binding values: approximate Ki from published studies, including the NIMH PDSP Ki database; values vary by assay.

Last updated October 2026. Pregnancy, lactation, and burden ratings are condensed judgments; verify against the sources before prescribing.
