Regimen burden

Select the patient's psychiatric medications. Bars stack each drug's contribution so you can see where side-effect load accumulates, and flags list the interactions that change management.

Cumulative burden

Each drug scores 0–3 per domain; bars are additive. "High" means a single drug at 3 or a combined score of 4+. A heuristic for spotting stacking, not a validated risk score.

Interactions & flags

Educational prototype covering five medications. Doses reflect FDA labeling for immediate-release adult/pediatric use unless noted and are abridged; check current labeling, formulation, and patient factors. Burden ratings are qualitative estimates informed by labeling, Huhn et al. 2019 (Lancet; antipsychotics) and Cipriani et al. 2018 (Lancet; antidepressants). Binding values are approximate Ki from published studies (e.g., NIMH PDSP Ki database) and vary across assays; affinity does not directly predict clinical effect.
